How I Build Solutions

I start with the work people do, where errors can enter and which decisions require judgment.

  1. Understand

    Map the process, pain points and risks.

  2. Simplify

    Reduce steps and define the business rules.

  3. Automate

    Build the workflow with data, automation and AI where useful.

  4. Control

    Flag exceptions and keep people in charge of decisions.

  5. Measure

    Track time saved, errors prevented and changes in the process.
