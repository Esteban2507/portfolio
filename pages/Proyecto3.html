Accounting Validation with VBA

Excel macros that prepare data and apply repeatable accounting checks.

Problem

The validation relied on manually preparing and comparing multiple accounting reports. Running the same checks again required repeating the preparation and comparison.

Solution

I used VBA to automate the preparation and cross-checking in Excel. The macros clean the data and apply the validation rules to the reports.

Technology

  • Excel
  • VBA

Result

The same checks can be repeated across new reports and differences are available for follow-up.

Impact

The workbook reduces repetitive steps and supports a more consistent review of accounting discrepancies.
