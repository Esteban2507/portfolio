Operations KPIs

A Power BI dashboard for tracking operational indicators in invoice intake.

Problem

Invoice intake teams needed a single view for looking at operational indicators and narrowing the data to the part of the process they wanted to review.

Solution

I built a Power BI dashboard using Excel inputs, Power Query and DAX. Filters let viewers change their scope and indicators draw attention to cases that may need a closer look.

Technology

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Excel

Result

The dashboard groups indicators in one view and gives reviewers filters for exploring the underlying activity.
