Executive Report Generator

A Spanish-language demo that turns sample notes, tables or bullet points into a structured executive draft.

Problem

Raw notes and figures need structure before a reader can navigate the key points, findings and next steps.

Solution

The app lets you choose a report type, tone and audience. It sends the supplied material to the selected AI provider and formats the response into sections such as findings, risks and recommendations.

Technology

  • React
  • JavaScript
  • AI provider APIs

Result

The generated draft can be copied as plain text for review and editing.

Try the demo

The demo is in Spanish and requires an API key from your chosen provider. The demo sends the supplied material to the AI provider you select. Use synthetic or public examples.
