Payment Control Automation

A Power BI control that compares payment records with supporting reports and brings discrepancies into view.

Problem

Before approval, I checked that the payee, issue date, document number, currency and amount matched the supporting information. Comparing records visually across sources took time and made consistent checks harder to repeat.

Solution

I built a Power BI dashboard that prepares the source files, compares the records and highlights discrepancies or possible duplicates for review.

Technology

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Excel

Result

The dashboard brings mismatches into one review view. I can verify the flagged differences and use the supporting documentation to decide how to proceed.

Impact

~60% Less time spent on the payment checks previously done by visual comparison

This is my estimate for the payment control workflow. The approver still reviews the evidence and makes the payment decision.
